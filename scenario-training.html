Mass Casualty Trauma Response

Most trauma training prepares you for one patient. This course prepares you for the moment there are multiple. Mass casualty events demand a different kind of decision-making, and Iron Rescue's MCTR course builds the triage instincts and coordination skills to operate effectively when the stakes are highest.

You'll run through realistic multi-casualty scenarios, practice rapid assessment under time pressure, and learn how to prioritize treatment when resources are stretched. By the end, your response is structured, not chaotic.

  • Rapid triage and casualty prioritization
  • Bleeding control on multiple simultaneous patients
  • Scene management and EMS coordination
  • Decision-making under high-stress, multi-casualty conditions

School Emergency Medical Care

Teachers and school staff are often the first adults on scene in a building emergency. This course is built specifically for educators and administrators who need to be ready to act when a school emergency unfolds, before emergency services arrive.

Iron Rescue's School Emergency Medical Care course applies TECC skills to the school environment: classroom emergencies, active threat scenarios, limited resources, and the added complexity of managing frightened students. You'll leave with TECC certification, a complete IFAK, and the confidence to lead in a crisis.

  • Life-saving interventions with limited school resources
  • Active threat medical response protocols
  • Managing multiple students in distress
  • TECC certification + IFAK kit included

Wilderness Medicine

When you're deep in the backcountry, help is not coming in minutes. It might be hours. Wilderness Medicine trains you to manage trauma and medical emergencies when evacuation is delayed, resources are improvised, and the environment itself is part of the problem.

You'll practice improvised wound care, fracture stabilization, hypothermia management, and patient packaging for extraction. Everything taught applies directly to the realities of hunting, hiking, and backcountry operations, where your knowledge and your pack are the only things between you and a bad outcome.

  • Wound management and hemorrhage control in remote settings
  • Improvised splinting and fracture stabilization
  • Environmental emergency management (hypothermia, heat, dehydration)
  • Patient packaging and extended care protocols

Home Emergency Preparedness

Emergencies don't happen at the range or in the field. They happen in kitchens, driveways, and living rooms, when the people involved are family members, not strangers. Home Emergency Preparedness applies trauma skills to the most personal scenario of all: responding to someone you love.

This course covers household trauma response, pediatric bleeding control, CPR/AED use, choking, and how to build and maintain a home trauma kit. It's also one of the most effective courses to take as a family unit, giving everyone in the household a baseline level of skill.

  • Trauma response for household injuries and emergencies
  • Pediatric bleeding control and airway management
  • CPR and AED training for cardiac events at home
  • Home trauma kit setup and maintenance

Workplace Safety and Response

Workplace emergencies, machine injuries, falls, cardiac events, sudden illness, often happen faster than any safety procedure can account for. Iron Rescue's Workplace Safety and Response course equips teams to handle the first critical minutes before EMS arrives, transforming your crew into a trained first response unit.

Available as a private team training for organizations of any size, this course is built around the specific hazards of your work environment. Easton customizes scenarios to your worksite so the training isn't generic, it's directly applicable to the risks your people face every day.

  • Workplace trauma response and injury management
  • Team coordination during multi-casualty incidents
  • CPR and AED operation in a workplace setting
  • TECC certification for all participants
