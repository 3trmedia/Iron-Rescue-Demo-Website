How It All Fits Together

The MARCH Algorithm

Every piece of gear above maps directly to a step in MARCH, the same casualty assessment order used by military and tactical medics.

M
Massive Hemorrhage
Tourniquet and hemostatic gauze to stop life-threatening bleeding first, before anything else.
A
Airway
Clear and maintain an open airway so the patient can keep breathing.
R
Respiration
Chest seal applied to any penetrating chest injury to keep lungs functioning.
C
Circulation
Check for further bleeding and signs of shock once the first threats are controlled.
H
Hypothermia / Head
Keep the patient warm and assess for head injury while you wait for help to arrive.
