Our Store (WordPress & WooCommerce)

This site is built on WordPress and processes class registrations and equipment orders through WooCommerce. When you complete a purchase, you'll receive an automated order confirmation email, and your order will be recorded in our system so we can fulfill it and provide support if needed. Product availability, pricing, and class seat counts are managed through this system and are subject to change without notice.

Class Registration & Payment

Full payment is required at the time of registration to reserve your seat in a class. Seats are limited and are held on a first-come, first-served basis. If a class reaches capacity, you may be placed on a waitlist.

Cancellations & Refunds

  • Cancellations made at least 7 days before a scheduled class are eligible for a full refund or transfer to a future class date.
  • Cancellations made within 7 days of a class are eligible for a transfer to a future class date, but not a refund, except at Iron Rescue's discretion.
  • If Iron Rescue cancels or reschedules a class for any reason, you will be offered a full refund or the option to transfer to another available date.
  • Physical products (such as trauma kits) purchased through our store may be returned unopened within 14 days of delivery for a refund, less shipping costs. Kits that were opened, used, or issued as part of a class are not eligible for return.
